    NOURISHMENT PRINCIPLES

    Fuel the body you're building

    ✦
    Protein across meals

    Fish, eggs, poultry, beans, lentils and other foods you enjoy help support muscle.

    ✦
    Fibre and variety

    Vegetables, fruit, peas, oats and whole grains; increase fibre gradually.

    ✦
    Calcium and vitamin D

    Consider suitable fortified lactose-free alternatives and other sources, especially for bone health. Homemade oat milk is usually not fortified.

    ✦
    Hydration that works for you

    Choose fluids you tolerate, watching added sugar in sweetened drinks.

    ✦
    Enough food and recovery

    Strength and body recomposition require nourishment; avoid turning meals into punishment.

    Homemade, lactose-free milk options

    • Oat milk: oats and water; useful for porridge and smoothies.
    • Almond milk: almonds and water; mild and nutty.
    • Soy milk: soybeans and water; generally provides more protein than oat or almond milk. Soybeans require proper cooking—don’t simply blend raw or soaked beans and drink.
    • Walnut milk: walnuts and water; a stronger, nutty flavor.

    Homemade milk’s nutrient content varies with the recipe, dilution, and straining. Keep nuts, seeds, beans, and other protein foods in your meals rather than relying on homemade milk alone.
