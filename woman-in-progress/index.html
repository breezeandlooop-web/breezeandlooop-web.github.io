A useful distinction

“This happened” is not the same as “this always happens to me,” or “this proves something is wrong with me.” You can acknowledge disappointment without using it as evidence against yourself.

Acceptance is not approval, surrendering your needs, or pretending something did not hurt. It is allowing reality to be real so your next move can come from clarity rather than self-punishment.
