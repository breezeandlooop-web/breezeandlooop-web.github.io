Add a financial entry

Use a consistent label for a debt and its repayments. Enter a debt balance once; don't repeatedly add the same balance. Transfers do not count as income. Trading P/L is separate from household cash flow. Currencies are never silently combined.

PLAN AHEAD

Bills, renewals & important dates

Car insurance, driver's licence, vehicle licensing, subscriptions and other commitments belong here. Add a due date and expected cost even before you've paid. When you pay, also record the payment as an Expense above; this avoids counting planned bills as spending.

GENEROSITY WITH INTENTION

Giving & helping others

Giving can be money, time, skills, practical help or kindness. Record monetary gifts as Giving / helping others in the financial register so they are included in cash flow. Non-monetary acts can be captured here.

Giving should remain voluntary and affordable. You can be generous without putting yourself under financial pressure.
