Pricing notes from the proposal

  • Prioritize weekly commercial and property-management contracts — they stabilize crew schedules.
  • Villa / vacation turnovers should include photo-verified inspection as a priced standard, not a free extra.
  • Kitchen extraction, floor care, and medical-grade cleans carry specialty premiums.
  • Project work (post-construction, move-in/out, events) is for cash injection and spare capacity — price for margin, not occupancy.
