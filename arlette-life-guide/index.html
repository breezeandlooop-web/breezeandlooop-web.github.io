          Who is Arlette

          You were born 1 July 1977 in Georgetown, Guyana — a coastal child under a Gemini Sun and Mercury, with Venus and Jupiter both resting in Taurus, Mars lit in Aries, Saturn in Cancer, and the Moon roughly in Sagittarius. No rising sign here: birth time unknown. What follows is your day-chart portrait — the sky’s vocabulary for how you move, make, and belong.

          Mind & voice — Sun + Mercury in Mithuna (Gemini)

          Your mind is dual, quick, and hungry for exchange. Storytelling, sales, hostessing, explaining the craft to a stranger — these come naturally. You can hold two ideas at once and make them charming. The soft spot: boredom when life goes flat, and the habit of showing unfinished work for a hit of dopamine or praise before the piece has settled. Your voice is a gift; train it to finish before it performs.

          Feeling & seeker — Moon in Dhanu (Sagittarius) ~23°19′ · Purva Ashadha

          You need meaning, horizon, and truth. A caged Moon in Sagittarius grows restless — travel, learning, or a bigger why restores you. Faith is not optional décor; it is how your heart breathes. When the week feels small, ask what larger story you are serving.

          Drive — Mars in Mesha (Aries)

          You start fire. Initiative is clean and brave. Impatience with slow steps is the shadow — you may leap past the boring middle that craft actually requires. Pair Mars’ spark with Venus–Jupiter’s patience in Taurus: begin boldly, then stay.

          Craft & dharma — Venus own-sign Taurus + Jupiter in Taurus

          Guru and Shukra share a sign: life-purpose spine. You expand through tangible beauty — coastal craft, objects with weight, values you can touch. Wisdom that stays in the head is incomplete for you; it must become matter. Breeze & Loop and maker life fit this spine: beauty with substance, not trend-thin glitter.

          Shell & belonging — Saturn in Karka (Cancer)

          Here sits the crab shell: caretaking as worth, approval as safety, fear of judgment or rejection. When you ask “am I people-pleasing?” — yes, as a Saturn–Cancer pattern. When you ask “am I looking for praise?” — yes, via Gemini mind (cleverness praised), Venus (beauty valued), and Saturn (belonging earned). These are skills to train, not curses. Secure belonging without self-erasure is your long work.

          Rahu in Kanya / Ketu in Meena

          Rahu in Virgo hungers for craft-detail, usefulness, and getting things right enough to serve. Ketu in Pisces asks for faith and release — not every seam needs to be perfect. The dance: useful precision without drowning in perfectionism; devotion without dissolving your edges.
